Your family’s setup guide

Set up each child’s club account, then get them into Minecraft or voice chat.

  1. Request to join.

    A parent or guardian fills out the join form and gives permission. A volunteer parent reviews the request and sends a welcome email after approval.

  2. Set up each child’s club account.

    Set your parent password from the welcome email, then open family setup to create each child’s account and connect their device. Save each child’s club username and password. You can come back and finish setup later.

    Parents manage their own children’s accounts, supervise play, and can read messages in shared rooms. Children don’t need an email address. See how parent chat works.

  3. Join the Minecraft server.

    Use Minecraft Bedrock. Save your child’s exact Xbox gamertag in family setup, then sign in to Minecraft with the matching Xbox account. Their Xbox gamertag is separate from their club username.

    Open Minecraft server details for the address, port and joining instructions. Sign in with your club account if prompted.

  4. Install Talk to chat while gaming.

    We recommend the Nextcloud Talk app for voice while playing. On a phone or tablet, a browser call will often drop when you switch to the game.

    1. Install Nextcloud Talk for Android or iPhone or iPad.
    2. Open Talk and enter talk.huskygaming.club as the server address.
    3. Sign in with your child’s club username and password. If a browser opens, choose Grant access and return to Talk.
    4. Open the Minecraft room or the room for your game and tap the call button. Allow microphone access when asked. Leave Talk running, then open your game.

    Just checking messages or making a quick call? Open the club in your browser and choose Open chat or Join voice in a room.

Parent oversight and safety

Who can join
A parent or guardian gives permission, and a volunteer parent reviews each request before the family gets access. Parents supervise their own kids.
Who can see chats
Parents can read messages in shared rooms. In Talk, direct chats are limited to their two participants. Parent-to-child direct messages stay within the family. Approved parents also have their own Parents’ chat. Club call recording is turned off.
What families share
We use your name and email for membership, setup, and account recovery. Club members can see children’s first names. Optional game usernames are shown to approved members in the matching private game room and on the private Play page.

Appointed volunteers may moderate room access. Email concerns about play or behavior to reports@huskygaming.club. For privacy questions, corrections, or account removal, email hello@huskygaming.club.

Frequently asked questions

How does my family join?

Husky Gaming Club is for Saint Clair Shores kids, friends, and families. A parent or guardian requests to join, then a volunteer reviews the request.

Where is my welcome email?

After approval, look for an email titled Your pack is welcome. Check spam or junk. The email has the link to set your parent password. If you still need help, email hello@huskygaming.club.

Does my child need an email address?

No. A parent creates a club account for each child in family setup. Each child signs in with their own club username and password.

Why can’t my child join Minecraft?

Open Minecraft server details and check the address and port. Use Minecraft Bedrock and make sure the Xbox account signed in to the game matches the exact gamertag saved in family setup.

Can my child talk and play on the same device?

Use the Nextcloud Talk app. Join the voice call in Talk, leave the app running, and then open the game. Browser calls often drop when a phone or tablet switches to the game.

How do we reset a password?

For a child, a parent signs in, opens the child’s name under Your pups, and chooses Reset password. This signs the child out on their devices. Parents can use Forgot password on the sign-in screen with their own email address.

Why are rooms missing in Talk?

Check that Talk is connected to talk.huskygaming.club and signed in to the right club account. Close and reopen Talk. If rooms are still missing, email hello@huskygaming.club.

Club rules

  • Be kind. Keep text chat and voice friendly. Leave out insults, bullying, and abusive language.
  • Keep personal information private. Keep your full name, home address, phone number, and passwords out of club chat.
  • Use family and parent chat. Parent-to-child direct messages are limited to the parent’s own family. To talk with other approved parents, sign in and choose Open Parents’ chat under Your pack. You can also open Parents’ chat in Nextcloud Talk.
  • Parents supervise play. Parents can read messages in shared rooms. Appointed volunteers may moderate room access.

If something happens that makes you uncomfortable, tell a parent or guardian. Parents can email concerns about play or behavior to reports@huskygaming.club.
