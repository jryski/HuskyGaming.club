YOUR FAMILY’S FIRST GAME NIGHT

From “hello” to “let’s play.”

Start with a parent. We’ll take it one step at a time.

  1. Ask to join, then watch your email.

    Choose Parents: Request to Join and send your name and email. Jesse reviews the request. After approval, your welcome email explains how to sign in. Check your junk folder if you’re waiting.

  2. Set up your family.

    Choose Member Sign In to open your family page. Under Your children, choose Add a child. Make a separate account for each child and keep their username and password somewhere safe. Parents manage these accounts.

  3. Choose a browser or the Talk app.

    Browser: sign in and choose General Hangout, Minecraft or Roblox. You can chat and call there without installing an app.

    Phone or tablet: install Nextcloud Talk using the buttons or QR codes here. Open Talk and enter this club address when it asks for a server:

    https://talk.huskygaming.club

    Sign in with that person’s club username and password. A browser may open to finish signing in; choose Grant access for the Talk app, then return to Talk. A child uses their own account.

  4. Find your friends and say hello.

    Open one of the three club rooms. Tap the call button for voice and allow the microphone when your device asks. For text chat, type a message and send it. Parents supervise play and help kids keep personal information out of chat.

A little help goes a long way.

Forgot a child’s password? A parent signs in, opens the child’s name under Your children and chooses Reset password. This signs the child out on their devices.

Forgot your own password? Choose Forgot password on the sign-in screen and use the parent account’s email.

Rooms don’t appear yet? Confirm the club address above, close and reopen Talk, and refresh. If you’re still stuck, ask Jesse for help.
