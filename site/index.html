Your family’s setup guide

Request → approval email → child accounts → play.
Already approved? Start at step 2. The Talk app is optional.

  1. Ask to join, then watch your email.

    Scan the New families code or choose Parents: Request to Join. A parent or guardian enters their name and email and gives permission. You can add your children’s first names and game usernames later. A volunteer parent reviews your request. After approval, look for the welcome email and check spam or junk if you don’t see it.

  2. After approval, set up each child’s account.

    After approval, use the welcome email to set your parent password and sign in. The guide remembers your pups and helps you create one account at a time, then connect that pup’s device. Save each child’s own club username and password for their device. You can stop and resume. Already a member? Continue pup and device setup.

  3. Playing Minecraft? Add the gamertag, then the server.

    Use Minecraft Bedrock. In your child’s club setup, enter their exact Xbox gamertag, then sign in to Minecraft with that same Xbox account. Their Xbox gamertag is separate from their club username.

    Open Play games: Minecraft server details for the server address, port and joining steps. If the gamertag is missing or wrong, a parent can add or edit it in that child’s settings.

  4. Want to chat or call? Choose a browser or Talk.

    Browser: sign in to the club and choose General Hangout, Minecraft or Roblox. You don’t need to install an app to chat or call.

    Phone or tablet app (optional): install Nextcloud Talk using the download buttons below. Open Talk and enter this address when it asks for a server:

    talk.huskygaming.club

    Sign in with that person’s club username and password, not their Xbox gamertag. A browser may open to finish signing in; choose Grant access for the Talk app, then return to Talk. A child uses their own account.

  5. Find your friends and say hello.

    In your browser, choose a room and tap Join voice—the camera stays off. Allow your microphone the first time your device asks. Tap the red phone button to leave. For text only, choose Open chat. In the Talk app, open your room and tap its call button. Parents supervise play and help kids keep personal information out of chat.

Stuck? Start with these checks.

Waiting for access? A request needs approval before the welcome email arrives. Check spam or junk. If you need help, email hello@huskygaming.club.

Forgot a child’s password? A parent signs in, opens the child’s name under Your pups and chooses Reset password. This signs the child out on their devices.

Forgot your own password? Choose Forgot password on the sign-in screen and use the parent account’s email.

Rooms don’t appear yet? Confirm the club address above, close and reopen Talk, and refresh. If you’re still stuck, email hello@huskygaming.club.
