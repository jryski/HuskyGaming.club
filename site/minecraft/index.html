MINECRAFT BEDROCK

A world we build together

The Friends world is a long-running Minecraft world. Make a home of your own, visit friends, and help grow a shared expandable city district by district.

Open Minecraft server details See the joining guide

Parent or guardian approval is required to join. Start with family setup if you’re new to the club.

Available in the world today

Friends Compass
Select the compass in your inventory and tap Friends. Pick Go to a friend to visit an online friend. Bring a friend sends an invitation they can accept.
Home bookmark
Use Save home here to remember a spot. Go home returns you to it. A bookmark does not claim land or protect blocks.
Starter city
Visit the city takes you to the starter plaza, with a town hall, two shop buildings, and wide streets.
Named destinations
Ancient portal is a bookmark for visiting a place in the Friends world. It does not open a portal or lead to another dimension.

What we’re building next

Modern city expansion
In development
We’re designing skyscrapers with a varied skyline, street-level shops, and interiors to explore. Wide streets will leave room for more districts as the world grows.
First-time homes
Planned
Place new players in a starting biome and save their first home bookmark automatically. Existing home bookmarks stay in place.
Badlands
Planned
Build a distant region of ruined settlements for rebuilding and battles, away from homes and the city.
